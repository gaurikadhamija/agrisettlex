About AgriSettleX

Restoring speed and trust across agricultural value chains

Agricultural trade often stalls between harvest and payment due to fragmented paper receipts, disputed quality grades, duplicate invoice pledges, and unpredictable weather delays.

AgriSettleX bridges the physical movement of produce with transparent digital verification—allowing every participant to confirm provenance, validate invoices once, and trigger timely settlement without friction.

Core Platform Capabilities

Six precision modules engineered for agricultural trust

Every module works together to verify harvests, protect invoice integrity, and accelerate settlement across the supply chain.

01

Crop-to-Cash Tracking

Follow every harvest batch from field weighing and transit milestones through final payout settlement in one clear timeline.

02

Invoice Verification

Cross-check weighbridge slips, lot grades, and buyer purchase orders before any invoice is approved for settlement or financing.

03

Smart Settlement

Automate split disbursements to farmers, transporters, and lenders the moment agreed delivery and quality milestones clear.

04

Weather Intelligence

Factor real-time rainfall, humidity, and transit route forecasts into harvest grading, storage windows, and delivery risk alerts.

05

Farmer Financial Passport

Build a portable record of verified harvests, fulfillment consistency, and settled trades to unlock fairer seasonal credit terms.

06

Duplicate Invoice Detection

Flag repeated invoice numbers, overlapping lot weights, and double-pledged receipts across participating financiers instantly.

How It Works

Four transparent steps from farm gate to final payout

A clear, auditable workflow ensures every stakeholder verifies their milestone without manual paperwork bottlenecks.

01

Harvest Lot Registration

The farmer records crop variety, weight, and origin moisture readings, anchoring the lot with a verified trade record.

02

Dispatch & Weather Check

Transporters confirm pickup and route handoffs while weather intelligence monitors moisture and transit conditions.

03

Invoice & Quality Audit

The buyer validates delivered grade and weight while AgriSettleX checks for duplicate or conflicting invoice claims.

04

Automated Smart Settlement

Once verified, funds settle cleanly across farmer, transporter, and financier accounts and update the Farmer Financial Passport.
